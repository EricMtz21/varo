# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo personal finance on mobile. A Spanish-speaking user who opens the app on their phone to log what they spent today, check their month-to-date balance, and make sure savings are on track. Sessions are short and intentional: one transaction added, one number confirmed, done. Desktop use is occasional and secondary.

## Product Purpose

micontaduria is a personal finance tracker that makes your financial picture legible at a glance. It tracks income, expenses, recurring transactions, and savings boxes across months. The tagline is honest: "Tus finanzas claras, decisiones inteligentes." Success means the user always knows where they stand, without friction.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, minimal, focused. The reference feel is Notion / Bear: calm, content-first, generous breathing room, no decorative chrome. Finance data is the content; the interface steps back and lets numbers speak. No celebration, no gamification, no anxiety.

## Anti-references

None stated. Avoid by default: corporate bank-app stiffness (BBVA/Santander), SaaS personal-finance chart-splash aesthetic (Mint/YNAB), and crypto neon-dark energy.

## Design Principles

1. **Data is the surface.** Numbers, labels, and amounts are the interface. Typography hierarchy and spacing serve legibility above all else.
2. **Phone-first, always.** Every interaction is designed for one thumb on a small screen. Desktop inherits; mobile defines.
3. **Quiet trust.** The app handles real money. It should feel steady and dependable without ever feeling corporate or cold.
4. **Nothing decorative.** If an element doesn't carry information or affordance, it has no place here. Restraint is the aesthetic.
5. **Spanish as native.** Copy, labels, and UX patterns are written for Spanish-speaking users, not translated from English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard good practices. Reduced motion already handled in globals.css via `prefers-reduced-motion`. No formal WCAG target specified, but contrast and legibility are non-negotiable given that numbers must read clearly at a glance.
